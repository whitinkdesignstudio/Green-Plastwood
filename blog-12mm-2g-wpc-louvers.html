Louvers

What can a 12 mm WPC louver do for a wall that paint can't?

Green Plastwood · SNG Eco India Pvt. Ltd.Updated October 20263 min read

Paint changes colour.

A louver changes dimension.

That distinction is important in modern interior design.

A painted wall gives you a new colour, but a louver can introduce:

  • Depth
  • Shadow
  • Texture
  • Rhythm
  • Repetition
  • Architectural detailing

The 12 mm 2G WPC/PVC louver range is designed for projects where surface treatment becomes part of the overall design.

What is a 12 mm 2G WPC louver?

WPC stands for Wood Plastic Composite.

Green Plastwood's product range uses WPC across multiple interior and exterior applications, with the company highlighting characteristics including water/moisture resistance, termite and borer resistance, weather resistance and recyclability.

The 12 mm 2G louver format provides another profile option for architects, interior designers and contractors.

Why does a louver change a wall?

Consider a blank wall.

There is no visual rhythm.

Now introduce repeated vertical profiles.

Suddenly the wall has:

  • Direction
  • Shadow
  • Repetition
  • Texture
  • Visual depth

This is why louvers can be useful when designing:

TV walls

They create a structured backdrop behind the television and furniture.

Bedroom walls

They can be used around headboards or selected feature areas.

Reception walls

In commercial spaces, a louvered wall can provide an architectural first impression.

Partitions

They can help define zones within larger spaces.

12 mm louvers and interior design

A good wall feature doesn't necessarily need to dominate the room.

This is where thinner louver profiles can be useful from a design perspective.

The goal can be to create a subtle texture rather than an overwhelming feature.

For example:

A minimalist office may use a neutral louver finish.

A residential living room may use a warmer wood-inspired tone.

A hospitality space may use a louver arrangement combined with lighting.

What should designers consider?

1. Direction

Vertical louvers can visually emphasise height.

Horizontal arrangements can create a different visual proportion.

2. Spacing

Spacing affects how dense or open the wall looks.

3. Lighting

Light and shadow can significantly influence the appearance of a louvered surface.

4. Colour

Colour should be coordinated with flooring, furniture and other wall surfaces.

5. Installation

The installation system and required substructure should be confirmed before procurement.

Green Plastwood's wider product catalogue includes WPC products for both interior and exterior applications. Its range includes:

How do you choose the right louver?

Before ordering, create a simple specification sheet:

Project: Residential / Commercial
Application: Wall / Ceiling / Partition / Exterior
Profile: 12 mm 2G
Required length: Confirm with supplier
Colour: Confirm available options
Quantity: Calculate area + installation requirement
Substructure: Confirm
Accessories: Confirm
Installation: Confirm

This simple process can reduce procurement mistakes.

Conclusion

The most interesting walls aren't necessarily the most complicated.

Sometimes a simple repeated profile is enough to create visual character.

The 12 mm 2G WPC/PVC louver is one option for designers looking to add texture and architectural detailing without relying solely on paint or flat surfaces.

Frequently asked questions

What are 12 mm 2G WPC louvers?

They are WPC/PVC architectural louver profiles designed for decorative wall and architectural applications.

Can 12 mm WPC louvers be used for feature walls?

Yes, they can be considered for feature wall applications.

Can WPC louvers be used on ceilings?

Green Plastwood's louver range includes wall and ceiling applications.

How do I select a louver?

Consider the application, profile, dimensions, finish, colour, installation system and project environment.
