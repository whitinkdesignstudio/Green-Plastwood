How Green Plastwood is made.

We make all Green Plastwood products in our own plants in Gujarat: mixing, extrusion, cutting, finishing, checking and dispatch. This page explains each step.

Our plants

We run three units, Unit 1, Unit 2 and Unit 3, at Tankara, Morbi district.

Together they produce about 1,300 metric tonnes a month.

Each unit has its own mixing, extrusion, cutting and finishing lines, and a store for finished goods.

Inside the plant

Our process, step by step

Nine steps from raw material to your site.

01

Raw Material Selection

Raw materials suitable for WPC are selected and checked.

02

Material Preparation & Formulation

Materials are prepared to the formulation for each product.

03

Mixing & Compounding

Everything is mixed into one even compound.

04

Extrusion / Profile Formation

The compound is extruded into frames, boards and door cores.

05

Cooling & Dimensional Stabilisation

The product is cooled so it keeps its size and shape.

06

Surface Treatment & Finishing

Surfaces are finished where the product needs it.

07

Cutting & Sizing

Products are cut to standard or order sizes.

08

Quality Inspection

Size and finish are checked before dispatch.

09

Packing & Dispatch

Packed and sent to dealers, stock points and project sites.

Quality checks

We check material before, during and after production.

Our Quality Control Framework

01

Raw Material Checks

Raw material is checked before production.

02

Process Monitoring

Key parameters are monitored during production.

03

Dimensional Inspection

Dimensions are checked against the specification.

04

Visual & Surface Inspection

Surface and finish are inspected.

05

Physical / Performance Checks

Physical checks as per product specification.

06

Batch Monitoring

Batches are monitored for traceability.

07

Final Inspection

Final inspection before packing and dispatch.

Who uses our products

WPC started as a door frame material. Today it is also used for doors, kitchens, wardrobes and office furniture.

Architecture & Interior Design

For architects and designers who need design flexibility with practical performance.

  • Interior partitions
  • Decorative elements
  • Wall applications
  • Furniture components
  • Interior structures
  • Custom architectural applications

Residential Construction

New homes and renovation, where moisture and termites damage wood first.

  • Bathroom & kitchen door frames
  • Bedroom and children room doors
  • Window frames
  • Kitchen cabinets & wardrobes
  • Bathroom cabinets

Commercial Projects

Offices, retail spaces, hotels and other commercial interiors.

  • Office furniture & workstations
  • Cubicles & partitions
  • Reception counters
  • Hotel room doors
  • Office doors

Infrastructure & Institutional

Institutional buildings, public infrastructure and large projects.

  • School & classroom doors
  • Hospital & clinic doors
  • Toilet doors that take daily wet cleaning
  • Government housing projects
  • Hospital furniture

Real Estate & Builders

For builders and developers with recurring construction requirements.

  • Same frame size and colour in every flat
  • Bulk supply to site
  • Custom sizes on bulk orders
  • Samples and test reports on request

Furniture & Fabrication

Boards, sheets and profiles that fabricators cut and fix like plywood.

  • Modular kitchens
  • Wardrobes
  • Office furniture
  • Decorative thru grills & wave boards
  • Laminated and printed panels

Doors & Fenestration

Where moisture resistance and dimensional stability matter most.

  • Single & double rebate door frames
  • Window frames
  • WPC solid doors
  • WPC 3 layer doors
  • Plain, laminate, texture & digital doors

Industrial Applications

Selected products for industrial and specialised uses, based on required specifications.
