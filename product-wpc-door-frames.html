About WPC Chaukhat

A chaukhat is the frame fixed in the wall that carries the door. It is also the part that fails first: it stands on a wet floor and it is the first thing termites reach. Greenplastwood chaukhats are made of wood polymer composite (WPC) at 750 to 1200 kg/m³, so they do not absorb water, swell or rot.

They are worked like wood. Your carpenter cuts, joins and screws them with the same tools, and the frame can be painted, polished or laminated. Choose single or double rebate, three profile models, four lengths and eight colours. Sizes and colours can be customised on bulk orders.

How To Choose A Size

  • Measure the finished wall thickness, including plaster.
  • Pick the frame width closest to it: 75, 100, 125 or 150 mm.
  • Pick 50 mm thickness for light doors and 63 mm for heavier or main doors.
  • Choose single or double rebate.
  • Choose the length: 7 ft for standard doors, 8 to 10 ft for taller openings.
  • Choose one of the 8 colours.

Have Questions? We've Got Answers.

Answers to common questions about WPC Chaukhat.

What sizes are WPC chaukhats available in?

Single rebate frames come in 75 × 50, 100 × 50, 100 × 63, 125 × 63 and 150 × 63 mm, plus 60 × 30, 65 × 40, 90 × 45 and 90 × 63 mm. Double rebate frames come in 120 × 50, 125 × 63 and 150 × 63 mm. Standard lengths are 7, 8, 9 and 10 ft. Sizes can be customised on bulk orders.

What is the difference between single and double rebate?

The rebate is the step cut into the frame where the door shutter sits. A single rebate frame has one step and takes one door. A double rebate frame has a step on both sides, so two shutters can hang in the same opening, for example a main door and a mesh door.

Which model should I choose: Mould & Groove, Mould & Plain or Square?

They change the look of the frame edge, not its strength. Mould & Groove has a rounded edge with grooves on the back face, Mould & Plain has a rounded edge with a plain back, and Square has straight edges for a flat, modern look.

Are WPC chaukhats suitable for bathrooms and kitchens?

Yes. Greenplastwood WPC chaukhats are 100% water proof and moisture proof, so they do not swell, rot or warp in bathrooms, kitchens or other humid areas.

Do WPC chaukhats need anti-termite treatment?

No. The frames are termite and borer proof and bacteria and fungus resistant, so no chemical treatment is needed.

What is the density of Greenplastwood chaukhats?

Chaukhats are made at 750 to 1200 kg/m³. In lab testing by Alekh Plastics Testing Centre (Nov 2022) a door-frame sample showed 0.90 g/cc density, 0.2% water absorption, a UL-94 V-0 flammability rating and 2236 N screw withdrawal force.

Which colours are available?

Light Ivory, Yellow Ivory, Pure White, Beach White, Teak Wood, Chocolate Brown, Coco Brown and Black. Other (NFC) colours can be made on bulk orders.

Can a WPC chaukhat be painted or polished?

Yes. The frame can be painted, polished or laminated, and polishing costs less than on wood. Regular re-polishing is not needed.

How is a WPC chaukhat installed?

The same way as a wooden one. The posts and head are cut, joined at the corners and fixed in the wall opening with screws, using normal carpentry tools.

Can sizes be customised?

Yes. Size, length, density and colour can be customised on bulk orders. Send us your drawing or requirement.
