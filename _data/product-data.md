### Frame installation steps (door frames and window frames)
1. **Cut The Posts And The Head**: Cut the two vertical posts to the opening height and the head piece to the opening width, allowing for the joint you will use. The drawings show the types of cutting for vertical and horizontal posts.
1. **Make The Corner Joints**: Join the head to the posts with one of the joints shown. Screw the joint; WPC frames are joined with screws.
1. **Fix The Frame In The Wall**: Place the frame in the opening, check both posts with a spirit level, then fix it to the wall. Keep the frame square so the shutter closes evenly.

### Overview: About WPC Chaukhat
A chaukhat is the frame fixed in the wall that carries the door. It is also the part that fails first: it stands on a wet floor and it is the first thing termites reach. Greenplastwood chaukhats are made of wood polymer composite (WPC) at 750 to 1200 kg/m³, so they do not absorb water, swell or rot.

They are worked like wood. Your carpenter cuts, joins and screws them with the same tools, and the frame can be painted, polished or laminated. Choose single or double rebate, three profile models, four lengths and eight colours. Sizes and colours can be customised on bulk orders.

Problem vs solution: **Wooden chaukhat**: Swells near water, Rots at the bottom on a wet floor, Gets termites → **Greenplastwood WPC chaukhat**: 100% water proof, Termite and borer proof, Fire retardant

**Advantages (grouped)**
- Water and weather: 100% water proof and moisture proof; Stands up to high moisture and humidity; Best to use as exterior-grade material; Bacteria and fungus resistant
- Strength and safety: Termite and borer proof; Fire retardant; High load-bearing capacity; Non-toxic and insect resistant
- Easy to work and own: Can be processed like natural wood and solid wood; Low polish cost compared with wood; Zero maintenance; Environment friendly, eco-recycle product

### Installation And Care
The steps from our PVC panel guide. Panels are pre-finished, so there is no polish or paint after fitting.
1. **Prepare the wall**: The plaster should be dry and flat. If the wall is damp or uneven, fix a light aluminium or WPC batten grid at 300 mm centres first.
2. **Fix the starting trim**: Fix the L-angle or J-trim along the skirting and the side corner with countersunk screws.
3. **Place the first panel**: Slot it into the starting trim and check it is plumb with a spirit level.
4. **Fix the edge**: Fasten the groove edge to the wall or frame with clips or fine countersunk screws. No screws show on the finished face.
5. **Interlock the next panel**: Slide its tongue into the groove until it sits tight, and repeat across the wall.
6. **Cut and finish**: Cut panels with a fine-tooth saw and finish the top and corner joints with matching edge profiles.
Care: Dust with a soft cloth or wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap. The surface is stain resistant and needs no polish, coating or touch-up.

### FAQs
**Q. What is a Greenplastwood recycled plastic sheet?**  
It is a solid sheet made from recycled plastic by SNG Eco India Pvt. Ltd., the company behind Greenplastwood. It is used mainly as a pallet in fly ash brick and paver block plants, and in place of traditional shuttering ply in construction.

**Q. Which sizes and thicknesses are available?**  
Brick pallets are made in 16, 18, 20, 25 and 50 mm thickness, in 600 × 600, 600 × 700, 800 × 850 and 950 × 750 mm. Paver block pallets are made in 8 and 12 mm, in 1220 × 1220, 1220 × 620 and 610 × 610 mm, plus 2440 × 120 mm as listed in our catalogue. Other sizes can be made on bulk orders.

**Q. Will the pallets work on my machine?**  
Yes. Our catalogue lists them as suitable for all machine types used for fly ash bricks: automatic, semi-automatic and manual. Tell us your machine and pallet size and we will confirm.

**Q. How long do the pallets last?**  
They are water proof, termite proof and rust proof, and in tests they showed no swelling after 72 hours of boiling and no delamination after 4 hours of boiling and 4 hours of air drying. They are made for long life and repeated use.

**Q. What is the 20% buy back?**  
Our catalogue offers a buy back at 20% for Greenplastwood pallets. Ask our team for the current terms when you order.
