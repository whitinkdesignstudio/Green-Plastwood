Common questions

What sizes and thickness do WPC solid doors come in?

Standard sizes are 27 × 78, 30 × 81, 33 × 84, 36 × 81 and 38 × 84 inches, in 17, 18, 24, 25, 28, 30, 32 or 35 mm thickness. Thickness, width, length and density can be customised on bulk orders.

Are WPC doors good for bathrooms?

Yes. WPC doors are 100% water proof and do not swell, rot or get termites, which is why they are the most common choice for bathroom and kitchen doors.

What is the density of a WPC solid door?

Green Plastwood solid doors have a density of 550 kg/m³ and can be made up to 750 kg/m³ on order. A lab-tested door & board sample showed 0.66 g/cc density, 0.2% water absorption, UL-94 V-0 rating and 65 MPa compressive strength.

Which designs are available?

Plain, carving, colour, laminate, texture (designs SG-001 to SG-006) and digitally printed doors (100+ catalogue designs). Share the design code when you enquire.

What is the minimum order for coloured doors?

Ivory is the regular colour. Other colours are made on a minimum order of 300 doors.
