About Recycled Plastic Sheet

Brick and paver block plants traditionally use ply, wood, metal or PVC foam pallets. Wood swells and gets termites, metal rusts, and both wear out with daily water and cement. Greenplastwood pallets are made from recycled plastic sheet that is water proof, termite proof and rust proof, so one set of pallets lasts far longer.

The same sheets replace traditional shuttering ply on construction sites. Our motto: recycle every day instead of throwing plastic away.

Have Questions? We've Got Answers.

Answers to common questions about Recycled Plastic Sheet.

What is a Greenplastwood recycled plastic sheet?

It is a solid sheet made from recycled plastic by SNG Eco India Pvt. Ltd., the company behind Greenplastwood. It is used mainly as a pallet in fly ash brick and paver block plants, and in place of traditional shuttering ply in construction.

Which sizes and thicknesses are available?

Brick pallets are made in 16, 18, 20, 25 and 50 mm thickness, in 600 × 600, 600 × 700, 800 × 850 and 950 × 750 mm. Paver block pallets are made in 8 and 12 mm, in 1220 × 1220, 1220 × 620 and 610 × 610 mm, plus 2440 × 120 mm as listed in our catalogue. Other sizes can be made on bulk orders.

Will the pallets work on my machine?

Yes. Our catalogue lists them as suitable for all machine types used for fly ash bricks: automatic, semi-automatic and manual. Tell us your machine and pallet size and we will confirm.

How long do the pallets last?

They are water proof, termite proof and rust proof, and in tests they showed no swelling after 72 hours of boiling and no delamination after 4 hours of boiling and 4 hours of air drying. They are made for long life and repeated use.

What is the 20% buy back?

Our catalogue offers a buy back at 20% for Greenplastwood pallets. Ask our team for the current terms when you order.
