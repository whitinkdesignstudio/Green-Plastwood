Common questions

Is a WPC board better than plywood for kitchens?

For wet areas, yes. WPC boards are 100% water proof, termite & borer proof and do not swell or shrink, while plywood absorbs moisture and needs treatment. WPC can still be cut, screwed, glued and laminated like plywood.

What thickness and size do WPC boards come in?

Thickness from 5 mm to 35 mm (5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 17, 18 … 25, 28, 30, 32, 35 mm) in the standard 8 × 4 ft size. Other sizes can be made on big orders.

What is the density of a WPC board?

450 kg/m³ to 600 kg/m³, customisable up to 750 kg/m³. A lab-tested board sample showed 0.66 g/cc density, 0.2% water absorption, UL-94 V-0 rating, 23 MPa flexural strength and 1565 N screw withdrawal force.

Which colours are available and what is the MOQ?

White, Red, Ivory, Orange, Coco Brown, Dark Grey, Yellow, Blue and Green. Minimum order 100 sheets per colour and thickness.
