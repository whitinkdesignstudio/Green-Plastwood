WPC chaukhat sizes explained: 75 × 50 mm to 150 × 63 mm

Green Plastwood · SNG Eco India Pvt. Ltd. · Updated September 2026

A chaukhat size is written as width × thickness in millimetres. The width should match your wall, and the thickness decides how heavy a door the frame can carry. Here is how to pick the right one from the Green Plastwood range.

All sizes at a glance

SizeTypeCommonly used for*
75 × 50 mm (3" × 2")Single rebateBathrooms, toilets, store rooms, light internal doors
100 × 50 mm (4" × 2")Single rebateBedroom and internal doors in 4" walls
100 × 63 mm (4" × 2.5")Single rebateInternal doors where a heavier door is used
125 × 63 mm (5" × 2.5")Single rebateMain doors and 5" walls
150 × 63 mm (6" × 2.5")Single rebate, square modelMain doors and 6" walls
120 × 50, 125 × 63, 150 × 63 mmDouble rebateOpenings with a door on both sides, e.g. a mesh door plus a main door
60 × 30, 65 × 40, 90 × 45, 90 × 63 mmSingle rebateSmaller openings, cupboards, special requirements
70 × 30, 120 × 50 mmWindow frameWindow chaukhats

*General guidance only. Confirm the right size for your wall and door with our team. Every size is available in 7, 8, 9 and 10 ft lengths. Density is 750 to 1200 kg/m³. Sizes can be customised on bulk orders.

Single rebate or double rebate?

The rebate is the step cut into the frame where the door shutter sits. A single rebate frame has one step and takes one door. A double rebate frame has a step on both sides, so you can hang two shutters in the same opening, for example a main door and a mesh door.

Mould & Groove, Mould & Plain or Square?

These are the three profile models. They change the look of the frame edge, not its strength.

  • Mould & Groove: rounded edge with grooves on the back face.
  • Mould & Plain: rounded edge with a plain back.
  • Square model: straight edges for a modern, flat look.

75 × 50 mm shown. Tap a drawing for exact dimensions.

Quick checklist before you order

  1. Measure the finished wall thickness, including plaster.
  2. Pick the frame width closest to it (75, 100, 125 or 150 mm).
  3. Pick 50 mm thickness for light doors, 63 mm for heavier or main doors.
  4. Choose single or double rebate.
  5. Choose the length: 7 ft for standard doors, 8 to 10 ft for taller openings.
  6. Choose a colour from Light Ivory, Yellow Ivory, Pure White, Beach White, Teak Wood, Chocolate Brown, Coco Brown or Black.
