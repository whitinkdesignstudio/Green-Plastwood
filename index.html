Our main product: the WPC chaukhat

We take care of the termite. You take care of your home.

Wooden chaukhats in bathrooms swell, rot at the bottom and get termites within a few years. A WPC chaukhat does not absorb water, so it keeps its shape and does not need anti-termite treatment.

We make single and double rebate frames in 12+ sizes, 7 to 10 ft long, in 8 colours. Carpenters cut, screw and polish them with the same tools they use for wood.

  • 30 years warranty*
  • 2236 N screw holding (lab tested)
  • Paint, laminate or polish
  • No termite treatment needed
