About WPC Window Frame

A window frame sits in an outside wall, so it takes rain on one side and sun on the other. Wooden frames swell, then shrink, then the paint cracks and termites get in. A WPC frame does not absorb water and is not attacked by termites or borers, so it stays the size it was fitted.

Greenplastwood makes two window frame profiles, 70 × 30 mm and 120 × 50 mm, in 7 to 10 ft lengths and the same eight colours as our door chaukhats. They are cut, screwed, polished or laminated like wood. Profiles, lengths and colours can be customised on bulk orders.

Have Questions? We've Got Answers.

Answers to common questions about WPC Window Frame.

What sizes are WPC window frames available in?

Greenplastwood WPC window frames are made in 70 × 30 mm and 120 × 50 mm profiles, in 7, 8, 9 and 10 ft lengths. Sizes can be customised on bulk orders.

Are WPC window frames good for exterior walls?

Yes. WPC is 100% water proof and weather and ageing resistant, and our catalogue lists it as best used as an exterior-grade material. It does not rot, swell or get termites like a wooden window frame.

Which colours can I choose?

Light Ivory, Yellow Ivory, Pure White, Beach White, Teak Wood, Chocolate Brown, Coco Brown and Black. Other colours can be made on bulk orders.

Can WPC window frames be painted or polished?

Yes. WPC is processed like natural wood: it is cut, screwed, polished or laminated, and polishing costs less than on wood.

What is the density?

Window frames are made at 750 to 1200 kg/m³, the same range as our door chaukhats. A lab-tested door-frame sample showed 0.90 g/cc density, 0.2% water absorption and a UL-94 V-0 rating.

How is a WPC window frame installed?

Like a wooden one. The frame members are cut, joined at the corners with screws and fixed in the wall opening, using normal carpentry tools.

Do WPC window frames need termite treatment?

No. They are termite and borer proof, so no chemical treatment is needed.
