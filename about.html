The people behind Green Plastwood

SNG Eco India Pvt. Ltd., Ahmedabad. Making long-lasting alternatives to wood since 2012.

“

Building trust through quality. Growing through innovation.

At SNG Eco India, we believe a business lasts only if what it sells lasts.

When we entered the WPC industry, we wanted to give India's builders a product that is strong, long-lasting and better for forests.

We started Green Plastwood with a long-term view: to be a supplier that builders, architects and dealers can depend on for years.

So we keep investing in machines, quality checks and people, and we judge every product by one question: will it still be working years from now?

As we grow across India, we will keep manufacturing responsibly and dealing openly with everyone we work with.

Thank you to our customers, partners and employees for being part of this journey.

Together, we are building stronger spaces and a greener tomorrow.

A journey built on material, trust & scale

Since 2012: three plants in Gujarat, 300+ projects, 50+ stock points and exports to 4+ countries.

  1. 2012

    The beginning

    SNG Eco India is established in Gujarat to make long-lasting alternatives to wood.

  2. 2018

    First national exhibitions

    Green Plastwood exhibits at India Wood 2018 and at Woodex Asia 2018, Gandhinagar.

  3. 2019

    Woodex Asia 2019

    Returns to Woodex Asia and receives a certificate of appreciation for participation.

  4. 2022

    Lab-tested quality

    Door frames, doors and boards are tested by Alekh Plastics Testing Centre, Ahmedabad.

  5. 2023

    ISO 9001:2015 certified

    The quality management system is certified to ISO 9001:2015 in October 2023.

  6. 2024

    India Wood and IGBC

    Exhibits at India Wood 2024, Bengaluru and becomes a member of the Indian Green Building Council.

  7. 2025–26

    Recognition and reach

    Gujarat Excellence 2026 recognition. Exhibits at GPBS 2025, Kolkata Wood 2025 and Intext Expo 2026.
