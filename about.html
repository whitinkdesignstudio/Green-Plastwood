A Message From Our Founders

“True growth is not measured by scale alone, but by the trust we earn, the standards we uphold, and the value we create.”

Every enduring business begins with a vision, but it is the commitment to quality, the trust of our customers and the dedication to continuous improvement that turn that vision into a lasting legacy.

When we began our journey in 2012, our objective was to explore better material solutions and create value through innovation, reliability and responsible business practices. Over the years, SNG Eco India Pvt. Ltd. has continued to build on this foundation and to expand its expertise in sustainable materials.

Through Greenplastwood, we deliver dependable WPC products for the construction, architecture and interior industries. Our focus has always been on consistent quality, stronger manufacturing capabilities and long-term relationships with our customers and business associates.

As we move forward, we remain focused on innovation, operational excellence and sustainable growth, guided by the same principles that have shaped our journey from the beginning.

We sincerely thank our customers, business associates, employees and well-wishers for being part of our journey. Your trust inspires us to aim higher every day.

Vision, Mission & Values

Where we are headed, what drives us every day, and the values behind every Greenplastwood product.

OUR VISION

Grow Into A Leading WPC Manufacturer

To steadily expand our manufacturing capacity, our product range and our reach across India and international markets, and to grow Greenplastwood into one of the most trusted names in WPC building materials, so that WPC becomes the natural choice over timber in every home and project.

More Capacity · Wider Reach
OUR MISSION

Make Building Materials That Last

To manufacture high-quality WPC products that replace timber, perform reliably for years and meet the real needs of builders, architects and homeowners, while we keep improving our processes, our service and our care for the environment.
