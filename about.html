Founder’s Message

“True growth is not measured by scale alone, but by the trust we earn, the standards we uphold, and the value we create.”

— Shailesh Patel & Gautam Patel, Founders, SNG Eco India Pvt. Ltd.

Every enduring business begins with a vision — but it is the commitment to quality, the trust of customers, and the dedication to continuous improvement that transforms that vision into a lasting legacy.

When we began our journey in 2012, our objective was to explore better material solutions and create value through innovation, reliability, and responsible business practices. Over the years, SNG Eco India Pvt. Ltd. has continued to build on this foundation, expanding its expertise in sustainable alternatives and modern material solutions.

Through GREENPLASTWOOD, we are committed to delivering dependable WPC solutions that meet the evolving needs of the construction, architecture, and interior industries. Our focus has always been on maintaining product consistency, strengthening our manufacturing capabilities, and building long-term relationships with our customers and business partners.

I believe that true growth is not measured by scale alone, but by the trust we earn, the standards we uphold, and the value we create for everyone associated with us.

As we move forward, we remain focused on innovation, operational excellence, and sustainable growth — guided by the same principles that have shaped our journey from the beginning.

I sincerely thank our customers, channel partners, employees, and well-wishers for being an integral part of our journey. Your trust inspires us to aim higher and do better every day.
