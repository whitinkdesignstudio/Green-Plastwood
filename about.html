Our story

Wood is still the default material for door frames in India, and it keeps failing in the same places: bathroom frames that rot at the bottom, doors that swell in the monsoon, furniture eaten by termites. We started Green Plastwood to make the same products in WPC.

We now manufacture from 3 plants in Gujarat with a production capacity of 4,00,000 sq. mtr. Our products are formaldehyde and lead free.

Our customers include Adani, IFFCO, Indian Oil, Intas, the MP Public Works Department and more than 300 building projects.

Why WPC is better for forests

A standard door frame uses a lot of hardwood. WPC replaces that timber, lasts longer, and can be recycled at the end of its life.

What this means in practice

Less timber cut, fewer replacements, and nothing toxic inside.

100%WPC products are recyclable at the end of their life.

What happens in the plant

Extrusion

WPC compound is extruded into frames, boards and door cores.

Cutting & sizing

Frames are cut to 7, 8, 9 or 10 ft; boards to 8 × 4 ft.

Checking

Size and finish are checked before dispatch.

Dispatch

Packed and sent to dealers, stock points and sites.

About SNG Eco India Pvt. Ltd.

SNG Eco India Pvt. Ltd. is the parent company behind Green Plastwood, a trusted Indian manufacturer of Wood Polymer Composite building solutions. Driven by innovation, sustainability and manufacturing excellence, the company produces WPC Door Frames, Boards, Sheets and Profiles for residential, commercial, industrial and institutional projects across India.
