Green Plastwood, a brand of SNG ECO INDIA PVT. LTD., is built around a simple idea — materials used in modern spaces should perform as beautifully as they look.

With more than 12 years of experience, we manufacture WPC door frames and a wide range of interior and exterior WPC solutions designed for durability, low maintenance and contemporary architecture.

From manufacturing and quality control to distribution and project support, our focus remains consistent — creating dependable materials that help builders, architects and homeowners build better.
