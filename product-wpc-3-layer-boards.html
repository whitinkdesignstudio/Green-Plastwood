About WPC 3 Layer Boards

Boards are made in 6 mm to 35 mm thickness in the standard 8 × 4 ft size, at 550–600 kg/m³ density (customisable up to 750 kg/m³), in White (standard), Ivory, Brown and WPC colours.

Key advantages

  • Three-layer build for a harder surface
  • Smooth finish for lamination
  • 100% water proof
  • Termite & borer proof
  • Fire retardant
  • Works with traditional carpentry tools
  • Formaldehyde & lead free
  • Eco friendly & recyclable

WPC 3 Layer Boards: common questions

What is a WPC 3 layer board?

A board built in three layers for a harder and smoother finish than a single-layer board, while keeping WPC’s water proof and termite proof properties. It is used for kitchens, wardrobes, office furniture and partitions.

What sizes are available?

6 mm to 35 mm thickness in the standard 8 × 4 ft size; other sizes on big orders.

Which colours are available?

White (standard), Ivory, Brown and WPC. Bulk orders start at 9 tons.

How is it different from a normal WPC board?

Normal WPC/PVC boards start at 5 mm and 450 kg/m³ density; 3 layer boards start at 6 mm and 550 kg/m³ with a three-layer build for a tougher surface.
