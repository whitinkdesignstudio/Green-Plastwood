About WPC Sheets & Boards

Plywood in a kitchen or bathroom takes in water at the edges, the layers open up and termites follow. A WPC board does not absorb water and has nothing for termites to eat, so cabinets under a sink or next to a shower stay as they were built.

The boards are worked like plywood. They are sawn, routed, screwed, glued and laminated with normal carpentry tools. Greenplastwood makes single-layer WPC / PVC boards from 5 mm and 3 layer boards from 6 mm, both in the standard 8 ft × 4 ft sheet, and both free of formaldehyde and lead.

Have Questions? We've Got Answers.

Answers to common questions about WPC Sheets & Boards.

Is a WPC board better than plywood for kitchens?

For wet areas, yes. WPC boards are 100% water proof, termite and borer proof and do not swell or shrink, while plywood absorbs moisture and needs treatment. WPC is still cut, screwed, glued and laminated like plywood.

What thickness and size do WPC boards come in?

WPC / PVC boards are made in 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 17 and 18 mm thickness, and 3 layer boards in 6 to 18 mm. The standard size is 8 ft × 4 ft. Other sizes can be made on big orders.

What is the density of a WPC board?

WPC / PVC boards are 450 to 600 kg/m³ and 3 layer boards 550 to 600 kg/m³; both can be made up to 750 kg/m³ on order. A lab-tested door and board sample showed 0.66 g/cc density, 0.2% water absorption, a UL-94 V-0 rating, 23 MPa flexural strength and 1565 N screw withdrawal force.

What is a WPC 3 layer board?

A board built in three layers for a harder and smoother finish than a single-layer board, with the same water proof and termite proof properties. It is used for kitchens, wardrobes, office furniture and partitions.

How is a 3 layer board different from a normal WPC board?

WPC / PVC boards start at 5 mm and 450 kg/m³. 3 layer boards start at 6 mm and 550 kg/m³, with a three-layer build for a tougher surface.

Which colours are available and what is the minimum order?

WPC / PVC boards: White, Red, Ivory, Orange, Coco Brown, Dark Grey, Yellow, Blue and Green, with a minimum of 100 sheets per colour and thickness. 3 layer boards: White is standard, with Ivory, Brown and WPC colour; bulk orders start at 9 tons.

Can WPC boards be laminated?

Yes. The surface is smooth and takes glue and laminate. Lamination is done as per your design.

Can a carpenter work with WPC boards?

Yes. They are sawn, cut and screwed with traditional carpentry tools and hold screws well.
