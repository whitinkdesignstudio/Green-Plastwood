Have Questions? We've Got Answers.

Answers to common questions about Greenplastwood products.

What is WPC and how is it different from wood?

WPC (Wood Polymer Composite) is made from wood fibre and polymer. Unlike conventional wood, Greenplastwood products are 100% water proof, termite & borer proof and fire retardant, so they do not swell, crack or rot. In lab tests our WPC showed only 0.2% water absorption and a UL-94 V-0 flammability rating.

Which products does Greenplastwood manufacture?

WPC Chaukhat (door frames), WPC Window Frames, WPC Doors (solid, 3 layer, texture and digital print), WPC Sheets & Boards (WPC / PVC boards and 3 layer boards), PVC Panel and, from SNG Eco India, Recycled Plastic Sheet.

Are WPC door frames suitable for bathrooms and kitchens?

Yes. Our door frames are 100% water proof, termite & borer proof and fire retardant, making them ideal for bathrooms, kitchens and humid environments, with no chemical treatment required against termites.

Can door frames be painted, laminated or polished?

Yes. Greenplastwood door frames are ready for paint, laminate & polish, like normal wood.

Do you supply across India and for bulk projects?

Yes. We supply across India through 50+ stock points and take bulk project orders with custom sizes and colours. Call or WhatsApp us for a quote.

What sizes do WPC door frames come in?

Single rebate frames from 75 × 50 mm to 150 × 63 mm, double rebate frames in 120 × 50, 125 × 63 and 150 × 63 mm, in 7, 8, 9 and 10 ft lengths. See the size guide.

Is there a warranty on WPC door frames?

Greenplastwood door frames carry a 30 years warranty*. Ask our team for the warranty terms for your order.

What is the minimum order?

For doors, Ivory is the regular colour and other colours need 300 doors. Boards need 100 sheets per colour and thickness, and 3 layer boards are made on bulk orders of 9 tons or more.
