FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is WPC wall cladding suitable for outdoor use? +
Yes, Green Plastwood WPC wall cladding is specially engineered with UV stabilizers and high-grade polymers, making it 100% weather-resistant and ideal for outdoor facades, balconies, gates and external feature walls.
Does it require regular maintenance? +
No. Unlike traditional timber, Green Plastwood WPC products never require painting, polishing, varnishing or sealing. Simply wipe down with water or mild soap as needed.
Can it be installed on existing walls? +
Yes, it can easily be installed over plaster, brick, concrete or drywall using standard aluminum or WPC framing substructures with concealed clips for a seamless finish.
Is it available in different colours and textures? +
Yes, we offer multiple architectural wood-grain shades including Teak Wood, Walnut, Oak, Grey Wood, Charcoal, and Sandalwood in both brushed and natural textures.
