Common questions

What sizes are WPC door frames (chaukhats) available in?

Single-rebate frames come in 75 × 50, 100 × 50, 100 × 63, 125 × 63 and 150 × 63 mm (plus 60 × 30, 65 × 40, 90 × 45 and 90 × 63 mm). Double-rebate frames come in 120 × 50, 125 × 63 and 150 × 63 mm. Standard lengths are 7, 8, 9 and 10 ft; sizes can be customised on bulk orders.

Are WPC door frames suitable for bathrooms and kitchens?

Yes. Green Plastwood WPC door frames are 100% water proof and moisture proof, so they do not swell, rot or warp in bathrooms, kitchens or other humid areas.

Do WPC chaukhats need anti-termite treatment?

No. The frames are 100% termite & borer proof and bacteria & fungus resistant, so no chemical treatment is needed.

What is the density of Green Plastwood door frames?

Door frames are manufactured at a density of 750 kg/m³ to 1200 kg/m³. In lab testing (Alekh Plastics Testing Centre, 2022) a door-frame sample showed 0.90 g/cc density, 0.2% water absorption, UL-94 V-0 flammability rating and 2236 N screw withdrawal force.

Which colours are available?

Light Ivory, Yellow Ivory, Pure White, Beach White, Teak Wood, Chocolate Brown, Coco Brown and Black. Other colours can be customised on bulk orders.

How are WPC door frames installed?

Frames are cut (vertical & horizontal posts), joined at the corners and fixed in the wall opening with screws, just like wood, using traditional carpentry tools.
